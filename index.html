The big picture in one table

Unit 2 comes down to two questions about any study: how were subjects chosen and how were treatments given?

Random assignment to treatmentsNo random assignment
Random sampleExperiment. Can conclude causation and generalize to the population sampled.Observational study. Can only conclude an association, but can generalize to the population sampled.
No random sample (volunteers, convenience)Experiment. Can conclude causation, but only for the subjects in the study (or those very like them).Observational study. Only an association, and only for the sample itself.
Memory hookRandom sample → generalize to the population. Random assignment → cause and effect.

Decision guide: what kind of study / sample is this?

Step 1: Did researchers impose a treatment?
No treatment imposed→Observational study|Treatment imposed→Experiment (check for random assignment)
Step 2: How was the sample chosen?
If the description says…It is a…
Everyone in populationCensus
Easy to reach / first 10 / people at one locationConvenience sample (biased)
People choose to respond (online poll, "rate us")Voluntary response sample (biased)
Label, random number generator or names in a hatSimple random sample (SRS)
Split into groups of similar people, SRS from every groupStratified random sample
Split into groups that each look like the population, pick some groups, survey everyone in themCluster random sample
Random start, then every k-th (k = N/n)Systematic random sample
